Privacy Policy

Welcome to eLupiHaler

eLupiHaler is a smart inhaler companion app provided by Lupin. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to support the app’s features and provide a seamless user experience:

  • Personal Information: Phone number, date of birth, gender, profile picture, and doctor’s details.
  • Device and Medication Details: Smart inhaler device ID and type, dosage, frequency, usage history, and alerts.
  • Location Data: Live location used for weather updates and air quality-based alerts.
  • Bluetooth Access: Required to connect and sync with the smart inhaler device.
  • Notification Access: Enables reminders, weather-based suggestions, and doctor communication alerts.
  • SMS Access: Used exclusively for OTP autofill during login and verification.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Enable core functionalities of the app
  • Track inhaler usage and health metrics
  • Provide weather-based medication alerts
  • Notify users with reminders and health tips
  • Communicate with your healthcare providers (with your consent)
  • Improve user experience and performance of the app

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your data to third parties. We may share your data only:

  • With medical professionals or caregivers authorized by you
  • With third-party services for analytics and weather updates
  • When required by law or to comply with legal processes

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures to protect your personal data, including encryption, secure data transmission, and access controls. However, no data transmission method over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You may exercise the following rights:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal information
  • Withdraw permissions such as location or Bluetooth access via device settings
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting our support team

6. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any significant changes will be communicated via the app or email.
