Account Deletion Information

We respect your right to control your personal data. This page will provide you with options to manage and delete your eLupihaler account and associated data.

What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following information will be permanently removed:

  • Personal profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Health and medication data stored in the app
  • Device connection history and settings
  • Usage analytics and progress reports
  • Trigger information and personal notes
  • Notification preferences and schedules

Before You Delete

Please consider the following before proceeding with account deletion:

  • Data Export: Download your health data if you want to keep a personal record
  • Alternative Options: Consider deactivating instead of deleting if you might return
  • Healthcare Continuity: Ensure you have alternative methods for medication tracking
  • Device Pairing: Your smart inhaler device will need to be re-paired if you reinstall the app

Deletion Process

Once this feature is available, you will be able to:

  • Request immediate account deletion through the app
  • Choose data retention periods for regulatory compliance
  • Receive confirmation of successful data deletion
  • Export your data before deletion (optional)

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for legal and regulatory compliance purposes, including:

  • Anonymized usage statistics for research and development
  • Transaction records as required by law
  • Safety and adverse event reports

Reactivation

Please note that once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you wish to use eLupihaler again, you will need to create a new account and reconfigure all your settings.
